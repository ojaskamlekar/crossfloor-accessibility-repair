\section{Named Procedures, Invariant and Proof Dependencies}\label{app:procedures}
The mathematical algorithm has no raw-state, cut-column or wall-clock cap.
Implementation caps interrupt enumeration and must return an incomplete-search
status. Floor order, collision cells, complete action--owner incidence units and
the event schedule are constructed, not assumed to be supplied decompositions.
Their cost belongs to $T_{rec}$. The geometry kernel uses metres, binary
floating-point predicates and $\epsilon=10^{-8}$; post-recognition discrete
optimization and decimal action labels are exact for that recognized contract.
This is not an exact-rational computational-geometry theorem.

\subsection{Invariant}
After an event, each retained history represents a complete original-action
prefix, every paid live footprint, mandatory introduced targets, and a separate
optional rooted witness per profile. Partitions are the connectivity induced on
selected live component positions and the permanent anchor after forgotten
positions are eliminated. No forgotten non-anchor witness block has closed.
The label is complete original cost, count and sorted IDs. Current-unit owners
remain explicit; completed-unit occupancy remains external until its floor
expires. Reduction preserves the least accepting label for every common future
continuation, rather than every individual prefix history.

\begin{lemma}[Recognition correspondence]\label{lem:r2-recognition}
For the recognized fixed-template class, available action sets satisfy the
kernel's unary/pairwise physical conditions exactly when their owned cells are
compatible.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Individually infeasible candidates are removed. Footprint boundary coordinates
partition each floor into covered cells; an action reserves all cells of all
its paid effect footprints. Same-owner occupancy shares a verified consistent
rectangle, while different owners cannot occupy one positive-area cell. The
recognizer explicitly checks agreement with compiled geometric action-pair
conflicts and rejects mismatches or unsupported tiny cells. Room geometry and
obstacles do not change with selection in this class.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Unit and floor forgetting]\label{lem:r2-forget}
Owner names may be erased only after their complete unit; an expired floor may
be forgotten only after its scheduled incident events and the non-anchor-block
test.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A future action reusing an owner would belong to the same incidence component,
contradicting completion of that unit. Its cells therefore become externally
occupied, not free. A unit beginning at floor $f$ has every effect endpoint and
footprint in the $L$-floor window. Every baseline edge is likewise scheduled at
its earliest endpoint. After the floor's incident events no future event can
touch it. A disappearing non-anchor selected block can never reach its root
later and is rejected; anchored connectivity is retained by the permanent root.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Stable full-action labels]\label{lem:r2-label}
Identical-state dominance and the grouped weighted basis preserve the original
cost/count/sorted-ID tie-break under a common continuation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each original action is processed once, so future IDs are disjoint from prefix
IDs. Costs and counts gain the same continuation contributions. If these tie,
the first differing ID of equal-cardinality sorted sets is unchanged by inserting
the same disjoint future set. Hence label order is continuation-stable. The
weighted basis uses exactly that order and groups only equal occupancy and equal
selected boundary sets; its product cut rows preserve joint profile completion
as established in Appendix~\ref{app:proof}, using the attributed rank machinery.
\end{proof}

Paid but unused effects remain in the stored ID set, cost and occupancy and are
materialized during final replay. No witness predecessor graph is required for
reconstruction. Empty demand sets admit the empty cost-zero repair because costs
are nonnegative; a vacuous profile may use a canonical harmless room anchor.
Source-equals-target demands likewise require no new edge. Multiple distinct
sources in one profile remain outside this rooted solver.

For input bit length $B$, a common decimal scale and the sum of original prices
have polynomial bit length: summing at most $m$ scaled numerators adds
$O(\log m)$ bits to their maximum length. Stored IDs require at most their total
input length and polynomial indexing. Exact label addition/comparison therefore
adds polynomial bit work. There are polynomially many original-action,
baseline-edge and floor events; resource domains contribute $(h+2)^{zL}$,
summed selected-set bases contribute $3^{prL}$, and witness branching and explicit
cut elimination contribute $2^{O(prL)}$. This derives every factor of
\eqref{eq:bound}, with preprocessing kept additive.

The supported proposition is a semantics-preserving physical/interface transfer:
feasible original action sets correspond to accepting owned-cell executions and
the least original label is preserved. Generic logic and CP-SAT can express the
same finite model. The logarithmic owner term is sharper than one displayed
linear-owner expression bound; a tighter resource formulation may match it.
Literature priority beyond this precise boundary remains unconfirmed.
